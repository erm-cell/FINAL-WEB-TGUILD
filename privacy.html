Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

Who we are

[t]guild (“we”, “us”) is a website design business based in Indonesia. This policy explains how we handle personal data when you visit this website or contact us.

What we collect

  • Details you give us: your name, company name, email address, phone number, your message and whether you want our newsletter.
  • Messages you send us on WhatsApp, email or social media.
  • Basic technical data, such as IP address, browser type and pages visited, recorded in server logs by our hosting provider (Cloudflare) so the site can be delivered and protected.
  • Your cookie choice, stored in your browser.

How and why we use it

  • To reply to your enquiry and prepare a quote (to respond to your request).
  • To deliver and manage services you buy (to carry out our agreement with you).
  • To send our newsletter, only if you opt in (your consent, which you can withdraw at any time).
  • To keep the website secure and working (our legitimate interests).
  • To meet legal and accounting duties.

Who we share it with

We do not sell your personal data. We share it only with providers that help us run our business: Cloudflare (hosting and security), Google (Google Fonts, which may receive your IP address when a page loads), WhatsApp/Meta and email providers (to talk with you), and any form or newsletter service we add later. We may also disclose data if the law requires it.

Transfers abroad

Some providers process data outside Indonesia or your country. When this happens we rely on providers that apply appropriate safeguards.

How long we keep it

We keep data only as long as needed for the purpose it was collected. Enquiries are kept while we talk with you and for a reasonable time afterwards. Newsletter details are kept until you unsubscribe. Client and invoice records are kept as long as the law requires.

Your rights

You can ask to access, correct, delete or restrict your data, object to some uses, withdraw consent, or receive a copy of your data. These rights come from Indonesia’s Personal Data Protection Law (Law No. 27 of 2022) and, where it applies to you, the GDPR. Contact us and we will reply within a reasonable time. You may also complain to your local data protection authority.

Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures, such as HTTPS and limited access, but no system is completely secure.

Children

This website is not aimed at children under 16 and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.
