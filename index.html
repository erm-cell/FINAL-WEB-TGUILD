Questions people ask us

Short answers about our websites, prices and timing.

Who is [t]guild for?

[t]guild is a web design business. GuildPack 1, 2 and 3 are websites for businesses. PersonalGuild is a personal branding portfolio website for freelancers, creators and professionals.

What does the free domain include?

Every GuildPack and PersonalGuild package includes your domain for the first year at no extra cost. You choose the ending you like, such as .shop or .tech, as long as the name is available.

How long does it take?

GuildPack 1 takes 3 working days. GuildPack 2 and GuildPack 3 take 5 working days. PersonalGuild takes 2 working days (Beginner), 3 (Intermediate) or 5 (Advanced).

How many revisions do I get?

GuildPack 1 includes 1 revision, GuildPack 2 includes 2 and GuildPack 3 includes 4. PersonalGuild includes 1 (Beginner), 2 (Intermediate) or 4 (Advanced).

How much is the yearly renewal?

For every GuildPack the renewal is $500 per year, paid in the month your website first went live. For PersonalGuild, the renewal price is agreed with you after you choose your domain.

Can my website have more than one language?

Yes. GuildPack 2 and PersonalGuild Advanced can add 1 extra language, and GuildPack 3 can add 2, with a language switch in the sticky header.
